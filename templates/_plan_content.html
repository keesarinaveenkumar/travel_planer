{{ ico('info') }}The budget per person is not accurate. It is only an estimate and can change according to the traveler — the season, how you travel, where you stay and what you buy. Getting to and from {{ place }} is not included.

How this estimate is made
  • Stay — {{ model.nights }} night{{ 's' if model.nights != 1 }}. Rooms are shared by 2 travelers, so bigger groups pay less each; a solo traveler pays for a whole room (or a cheaper single or dorm bed). A room costs about a night.
  • Food — three meals plus snacks, about per person per day.
  • Local transport — the rides in your itinerary plus daily hotel transfers. Cabs and autos are split between up to 4 travelers; buses, trains and flights are paid per person.
  • Entry & activities — ticket prices from your itinerary (Indian-citizen rates).
  • Buffer — 10% for shopping, tips and small surprises.
